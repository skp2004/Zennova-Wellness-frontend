THE BLEND

What's in the mix

No single grain does everything well — so we don't rely on one. Each grain brings something different to your roti.

01

Wheat

The everyday foundation. Provides the gluten structure that makes rotis soft, pliable and easy to roll.

Structure & Softness
02

Jowar (Sorghum)

High in fibre and gluten-light. Adds a mild earthy depth and supports healthy digestion.

Fibre · Gluten-light
03

Bajra (Pearl Millet)

Rich in iron and magnesium. Provides slow-release energy that keeps you full longer throughout the day.

Iron · Slow Energy
04

Ragi (Finger Millet)

One of nature's best plant sources of calcium. Ideal for growing children and bone health at every age.

Calcium-rich
05

Chana (Bengal Gram)

Protein-packed legume that adds a heartier bite and rounds out the amino acid profile of the blend.

Protein · Satiety
HOW WE MAKE IT

From farm to flour

A short supply chain, on purpose. We believe what's left out matters as much as what goes in.

01

Source

Grains bought directly from regional farms and cleaned by hand. We know where every grain comes from.

02

Blend

Wheat, jowar, bajra, ragi and chana measured to a fixed ratio — the same every single batch, no shortcuts.

03

Stone-grind

Milled slow and cool on traditional stone chakki so heat never destroys the nutrients in the germ and bran.

04

Pack & ship

Sealed within 48 hours of grinding with the milling date stamped on every bag. Shipped direct to your door.
